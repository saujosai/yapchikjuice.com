Contact Yapchik Juice

Fresh-squeezed, small-batch, almost fictional

Got a joke, a gripe, or a half-baked invention in search of a punchline? Send it my way.

shaul@yapchikjuice.com

What to send

Feedback. Which strips made you laugh out loud, which ones got a polite chuckle, and which ones missed entirely. Honest reactions are more useful than polite ones.

Idea submissions. A character, a situation, a line you heard at the deli and can't stop thinking about. Rough is fine — finding the punchline is half the job.

Other discussions. Jewish humor, frum comics, and the oddly specific world in between. If it's funny and it's ours, I want to hear it.

What happens next

I read every message. I can't turn every idea into a strip, but the good ones go straight into the queue — and sometimes a single line turns into a whole batch of juice.
