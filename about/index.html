About Yapchik Juice

Fresh-squeezed, small-batch, almost fictional

The goal is to make you laugh and give you a boost to make it through your day's avoda.

Where the name came from

In Zanvil Feldman's audio story The Shmodster, two goyish fundraisers walk into Dovid's Deli. One of them orders herring juice. The man behind the counter doesn't miss a beat.

“We're out of herring juice. But we've got yapchik juice.”
— the deli counter

Who squeezes the juice

Yapchik Juice is written by Shaul Saitowitz and illustrated by AI. He always had comic ideas floating around in his head, but didn't have the art skills to execute them. Seeing TheFrumside.com comics using AI art showed him it could be ok.

Yapchik Juice started as a branch off his other project, ParnasaMatch.com. Unable to afford advertising, he decided to make his own.

Parnasa Match connects frum job seekers with insiders at companies who can refer them for openings, for free. Please consider taking 30 seconds to sign up as a referrer if you work at any company with a career page. And check out what's available if you are looking for your next opportunity.
